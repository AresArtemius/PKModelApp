1. SCREEN RECORDING
Attached (recorded on iPhone, iOS 26). It starts with launching the app and shows: sign-up with a new e-mail account, sign-in, the feed with user-generated posts, reporting a post and a comment from the "…" menu, blocking a user from the account page, the catalogue with filters, a model profile, the castings list and applying to a casting, chats, and finally account deletion from "My account" → "Delete account".

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
PK Management is a B2B working tool for the modelling and advertising industry in Russia. Users are models and actors (profiles of minors are managed by a parent or legal guardian), photographers and other creative professionals, and clients: casting directors, advertising agencies, brands and production companies. The problem it solves: today castings are run through messengers and spreadsheets. The app gives one place for a verified catalogue of talent, castings with applications, selections (shortlists) that a client can review, direct chats and a professional feed. All public profiles are reviewed by our moderators before they become visible.

3. SET-UP AND ACCESS
No set-up is required. Sign in with the demo account provided in App Review Information (e-mail + password). The demo account has the "client" role: the catalogue, castings, feed, chats and selections are available immediately. To see the model side, you can register a new account with any e-mail (no verification code is needed for review) and create a profile; it will be visible to moderators only until approved. Sign in with Apple is also available on the login screen.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (supabase.com): authentication (e-mail/password, phone, Sign in with Apple), PostgreSQL database and file storage for photos, videos and voice messages.
- Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): push notifications only.
- Sentry: crash reporting.
- Apple "Sign in with Apple".
No advertising SDKs, no analytics trackers, no AI services, no payment processors in the iOS app. The app does not sell anything: listing a profile in the catalogue is a service purchased on our website; the app only shows the current listing status.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app functions identically in all regions. The interface language follows the device language (Russian or English).

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app does not operate in a regulated industry. All photos and videos are uploaded by the users themselves (or by the parent/guardian for a minor) and, under our Terms of Use, users confirm they hold the rights to the material and consent to its publication. Every public profile is checked by a human moderator before publication. Users can report any profile, post, comment or message from the "…" menu and block other users; reported content is reviewed within 24 hours. Child-safety standards: https://app.pk.management/#/child-safety. Privacy policy: https://app.pk.management/#/privacy.
