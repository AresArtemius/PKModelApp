GUIDELINE 1.2 — User-Generated Content
Blocking abusive users is now available directly from the content: every post's "…" menu has "Block author", and every account page has "…" → "Block". After confirmation:
- all posts and comments of the blocked user are removed from the blocker's feed instantly (no reload needed), and the server excludes them from every later load;
- the blocked user can no longer message, follow or comment to the blocker;
- our moderation team receives an automatic notification about every block (in-app and push) and reviews the blocked account within 24 hours, removing content and suspending the account where appropriate.
Flagging: posts ("…" → Report), comments (flag icon), accounts ("…" → Report) and chat messages can be reported with a reason; the user sees a confirmation that moderators will review it within 24 hours.
Terms: the registration form cannot be submitted without accepting the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Child Safety Standards and data-processing consent; the documents open from the links under the switch and from the login screen.
The attached screen recording (physical iPhone, latest iOS) shows the terms step, flagging a post and a comment, and blocking an author with the posts disappearing immediately.

GUIDELINE 2.1(b) — Business model
The iOS app sells nothing and contains no In-App Purchases, paywalls, prices or links to purchase. PK Management is a modelling agency; the app is used by the agency's own models and by its partners (casting directors, brands, photographers). Some features (chats and invitations) are available to accounts with the "Pro" status. This status is not a paid subscription and cannot be bought anywhere — it is granted manually and free of charge by the agency's administrators to its models and partners, the same way the agency admits people to its database. The wording in the previous build ("plan", "upgrade") was misleading and has been replaced: the app now explains that the status is granted by the agency and offers to contact support.
1. Who uses paid subscriptions in the app? Nobody: there are no paid subscriptions. The Pro status is an agency membership status granted by administrators, not a purchase.
2. Where can users purchase subscriptions accessed in the app? Nowhere. The Pro status cannot be purchased on any platform. Our website sells one separate web-only service — publishing a model profile in the web catalogue — which is not sold, advertised or linked from the app and does not unlock any app feature.
3. What previously purchased subscriptions can a user access in the app? None. The "Listing status" screen only informs a model whether their profile is currently published in the web catalogue; it unlocks nothing and contains no prices or purchase links.
4. What paid content, subscriptions or features are unlocked without In-App Purchase? None.
5. Are the enterprise services sold to single users, consumers or families? They are not sold at all: the Pro status is assigned by the agency to its own models and business partners.
The review account (art@test.ru) already has the Pro status, so every feature is available for testing. If we ever add paid features to the iOS app, they will be offered through In-App Purchase.
