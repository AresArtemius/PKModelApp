GUIDELINE 1.2 — User-Generated Content
Blocking abusive users is now available directly from the content: every post's "…" menu has "Block author", and every account page has "…" → "Block". After confirmation:
- all posts and comments of the blocked user are removed from the blocker's feed instantly (no reload needed), and the server excludes them from every later load;
- the blocked user can no longer message, follow or comment to the blocker;
- our moderation team receives an automatic notification about every block (in-app and push) and reviews the blocked account within 24 hours, removing content and suspending the account where appropriate.
Flagging: posts ("…" → Report), comments (flag icon), accounts ("…" → Report) and chat messages can be reported with a reason; the user sees a confirmation that moderators will review it within 24 hours.
Terms: the registration form cannot be submitted without accepting the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Child Safety Standards and data-processing consent; the documents open from the links under the switch and from the login screen.
The attached screen recording (physical iPhone, latest iOS) shows the terms step, flagging a post and a comment, and blocking an author with the posts disappearing immediately.

GUIDELINE 2.1(b) — Business model
The iOS app contains no paid content, subscriptions or features, and no paywalls. In the previous build a few features (chats, selections) checked a subscription that exists only in our web product; this has been removed — in the iOS app every signed-in account has the full feature set. Answers to your questions:
1. Who uses paid subscriptions in the app? Nobody: the iOS app has no paid subscriptions or paid features.
2. Where can users purchase subscriptions accessed in the app? Nowhere; nothing in the iOS app is unlocked by a purchase. Our website sells a separate web-only service (listing a model profile in the web catalogue); it is not sold, advertised or linked from the iOS app.
3. What previously purchased subscriptions can a user access in the app? None. The "Listing status" screen only informs a model whether their profile is currently published in the catalogue; it does not unlock anything and contains no prices or purchase links.
4. What paid content or features are unlocked without In-App Purchase? None.
5. Are enterprise services sold to single users, consumers or families? Not applicable: the iOS app does not sell or unlock any services.
If we ever add paid features to the iOS app, they will be offered through In-App Purchase.
